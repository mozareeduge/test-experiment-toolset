# Voice patterns — bounded extraction, not the whole corpus

Concrete precedent for what "carries Mohammad's writing intelligence" looks
like, pulled from mozare-wiki records. This is a bounded set from two
extraction passes (an initial 3-entry pass, then a second pass over his own
authored works — MA thesis, the play *Grave*/گور, and the short-story
collection *Blood-Jetting*/*Elements of Affliction* — plus one raw planning
document), not a survey of the full ninety-four-artifact corpus. Grow it
opportunistically when a task already has a wiki source open; don't
schedule a dedicated rediscovery pass beyond what's here. When mozare-wiki
is reachable, prefer reading the live source files directly over this
snapshot — the entries below name their exact source path for that reason.

## The canonical before/after (protocol.md's own source)

> Bad: "The profile mediates capta through ontographic relation."
> Good: "A deleted sentence should not enter the system as ordinary text. It
> must arrive with the fact of deletion still attached to it. I call this
> structured research surface a profile…"

The move: a small, concrete, almost narratable action (a sentence gets
deleted; the deletion has to travel with it) *earns* the technical term
before the term does any work. Object first, then the name for what the
object needs.

## Holding heterogeneous names beside each other

From `03-objects/works/the-black-bird.md`, describing the poem's object:

> "The bird does not enter as one stable symbol. It becomes available
> through distinct names and conditions: crow, raven, blackbird, ghurāb,
> hrafn, scald-crow, messenger, witness, behavioral subject, scriptural
> figure, source-language problem, and poetic shadow." — immediately
> followed by: "These names do not simply decorate one underlying figure.
> Each changes what may be followed."

The move is not a list for its own sake — it's a list followed by a sentence
that states what the plurality *does*, so the enumeration earns its place
instead of standing in for argument. This is the sanctioned use of a list
under "turn terms into relations": list, then say what the listing changes.

## Recurring operations, not one thesis

From `04-notes/research/mozare-practice-constellation.md`, refusing a single
causal narrative across dramatic writing, translation, curatorial work,
research poetry, and research architecture — naming recurring *operations*
instead: holding heterogeneous objects together, preserving transformation
(draft/translation/runtime/revision kept distinct, not collapsed), resisting
smooth closure, making as inquiry, translating across domains through a
named shared operation.

Two consequences for drafting:

1. Connecting two of Mohammad's projects or fields needs a *named shared
   operation* ("in the archive, the problem appears as...; in interface
   design, the structurally similar problem appears as...") — never thematic
   association alone.
2. Don't resolve a tension the source material keeps open (e.g., "a
   reservoir preserves abundance; a proposal must select") by silently
   picking a side — name the tension and the local reason a given output
   resolves it one way.

## Operational modes instead of one essentializing definition

From `03-objects/works/ma-thesis-chapter-3-english-translation.md`
(`mw-work-ma-thesis-chapter-3-translation`) — a genuine English translation
of his own MA-thesis chapter, not a wiki profile written about his work.
The chapter "begins by questioning the persistent treatment of ugliness as
beauty's deficient opposite," then instead of supplying a replacement
definition, follows "several operations through which ugliness becomes
productive inside art: the sublime; resistance to the organism;
formlessness; fragmentation; the grotesque; the uncanny." `ma-thesis.md`
names the same choice directly: "The purpose is not to produce one
universal definition. It is to identify operations that can be tested."

The move: when a received opposition (ugly/beautiful) doesn't hold up, he
doesn't propose a cleaner opposition to replace it — he replaces the
opposition with a set of operations the material actually performs. This is
the scholarly-register version of "characterize before contrast," one level
up: contrast a *received binary itself* by dissolving it into operations,
rather than arguing for a different binary.

## Recurring apparatus instead of linear throughline

From `03-objects/works/grave.md` (`mw-work-grave`, the play گور/*Goor*,
practical component of the MA thesis) — the play is built from eleven named
pieces, three of them recurring "Entrance to the Machine" scenes that "do
more than separate scenes. They reopen the handling apparatus and return
other dramatic fields to corpse, paper, repetition, and unfinished
processing." The page names this precisely: "continuity is therefore
produced by returning pressures and object chains rather than by one stable
protagonist or linear plot," and one material rule runs under it —
"corpse and paper are handled together... the same work returns, but the
return accumulates pressure rather than completing a task."

The move: a long or dense text doesn't need one argument carried start to
finish. It can be organized around a small set of material operations that
recur and accumulate pressure each time they return — closer to how a
reservoir or a theoretical section should actually work than a forced
single throughline. Directly extends `mozare-practice-constellation.md`'s
"resisting smooth closure" with a concrete structural device rather than a
general disposition.

## Evidentiary discipline applied to his own fiction, not just citations

From `03-objects/works/blood-jetting-elements-of-affliction.md`
(`mw-work-blood-jetting`, the seven-story Persian collection خون
جهنده/عناصر ابتلا) — the page carries an explicit heading, "What is
explicit, and what is interpretation," separating what the seven stories
literally repeat (thresholds, obstructions, substitutions among animal,
corpse, costume, witness, writer, document) from what a systemic reading of
them would claim. It also preserves a withdrawn claim as **residue** rather
than deleting it: "Spider does **not** identify a dagger as the murder
weapon. The former 'dagger murder method' statement is withdrawn and
retained only as corrected residue in the audit history."

The move: this is `protocol.md`'s source-and-citation discipline and
`wiki-records.md`'s residue rule, both showing up unprompted in how he
catalogues his *own* creative writing — evidentiary care isn't a special
mode switched on for academic citation, it's a general compositional
stance. A claim that turns out wrong gets corrected and kept visible as
residue, not silently erased. Use this as the concrete precedent when
`mozare-critic` or `mozare-finalize` needs an example of what "mark it,
don't erase it" actually looks like in his own hand.

## What rough input from him actually looks like

From `_originals/MY CV AS I READ IT.txt` — not a finished text, planning
notes written to think through how to present a hybrid background for
cold-emailing potential PhD supervisors. Two things worth modeling
precisely, because this is raw material, not polished prose:

1. **The bridging move, done naturally.** He connects an MA in Dramatic
   Literature ("theories of literature, dramatic literature, theories of
   Art... many humanities disciplines") with a BSc in Industrial
   Engineering and product-management experience ("courses on a majority of
   engineering disciplines to find a multi-perspective vision") — two
   competencies stated side by side, each with its own concrete grounding,
   not blended into one vague "interdisciplinary" claim. This is
   `protocol.md`'s interdisciplinarity rule in its rawest form, before any
   polish.
2. **Flagged uncertainty, stated plainly.** He closes with "BUT I AM NOT
   SURE OF ACADEMIC WEIGHT OF 3 and 4" — not hedged into vague prose, just
   stated as an open question. `protocol.md`'s rough-input-handling rule
   ("classify speculative material rather than presenting it as settled")
   exists to carry exactly this kind of flagged uncertainty *forward* into
   the draft as an explicit question or a careful assumption — never to
   quietly resolve it one way and erase that he was unsure.

## What this file is for

Calibrate register before drafting; check a finished paragraph against a
concrete precedent instead of only an abstract rule. Not a style dictionary
to imitate phrase-by-phrase — the target is reasoning the way Mohammad does
from new material, not recycling old vocabulary because it's familiar.
